Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript "Prediction-augmented, payload-wind-aware multiple-observer control of a quadrotor with a
slung load in measured wind" for consideration as a Research Article in the International Journal of Dynamics and
Control.

The manuscript addresses position control of a quadrotor that carries a cable-suspended load in wind. It argues that,
once the wind is measured, the error that remains under an observer-based anti-disturbance controller is set by two
gaps in the controller's model: the loop delay acts on a payload force that is periodic along a planned trajectory,
and the wind feed-forward omits the drag on the load. Both gaps are closed with information the controller already
holds. The disturbance-observer estimate is propagated over the loop delay through the observer's own exosystem, and
the measured-wind feed-forward is scaled by the load's share of the wind force given by the force balance of the
coupled system. A boundedness argument shows that neither addition can destabilise a baseline that is input-to-state
stable with respect to its compensation error.

The evaluation is a simulation study of a quadrotor coupled to a spherical pendulum in wind measured at a
meteorological tower. Every claim was registered before its data were opened and was tested on fourteen held-out
days: the delay compensation lowers the error of the measured-wind baseline by 58 %, and the payload-drag term lowers
it by a further 63 % in hover and 37 % on a circle. The work concerns the dynamics and control of a nonlinear,
underactuated system under measured disturbances, which we believe fits the scope of the journal.

The manuscript is prepared for double-anonymous review; the author names, affiliations and declarations are given in
the submission system. The work has not been published and is not under
consideration elsewhere, and all authors have approved the submission. No funding was received, and the authors have
no competing interests to declare. AI-assisted tools were used to help write the simulation and analysis code and to
